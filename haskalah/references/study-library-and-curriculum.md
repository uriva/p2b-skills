# Haskalah Study Library & Curriculum (`https://haskalah.pages.dev/`)

Complete catalog of the free courses, lessons, and downloadable PDF/EPUB textbooks on `https://haskalah.pages.dev/` (licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0, derived from Hebrew Wikibooks and curated for independent learners).

---

## Pedagogical Approach: How to Guide Learners

1. **Treat Learning as a Goal-Oriented Menu:** Do not overwhelm users with the entire 1,253-lesson catalog at once. Ask what they want to achieve (e.g., "understand everyday English", "manage a budget", "pass Bagrut math for university", "learn how computers and privacy work", "understand biology and evolution") and point them to 1–2 starting resources.
2. **Offer Both Interactive Lessons and Offline Books:**
   - Users with steady internet can study lesson-by-lesson in the web tracks (`/core/`, `/digital/`, `/science/`, `/math-advanced/`).
   - Users who need **offline access** (for privacy, commuting, or limited connectivity) should download the **PDF or EPUB** textbooks from `/download/` to their phone or e-reader.
3. **Interactive Tutoring:** Whenever a user studies a topic from the library, offer to act as their personal tutor (*חברותא* / מורה פרטי)—explaining concepts in plain Hebrew, giving practice exercises, and connecting abstract ideas to everyday examples they already know.
