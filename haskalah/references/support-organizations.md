# Support Organizations & Aid Directory (יש למי לפנות)

Verified contact directory from `https://haskalah.pages.dev/support/` (updated April 2026) and the *"חוזרים בתבונה"* course (`references/transition-and-independence-guide.md`). Share relevant contacts proactively whenever a user faces a personal, emotional, legal, financial, medical, or safety challenge.

---

## 1. Haredi Transition, Shelter, Legal Jurisdiction & Pre-Military Programs

- **חוזרים בתבונה / ההשכלה (Haskalah Course & Host Volunteer Network)**
  - **Role:** Free core curriculum, transition course, and matching with volunteer secular host families across Israel.
  - **Website:** `https://haskalah.pages.dev/` | Course: `https://haskalah.pages.dev/course/`
- **הלל — האגודה ליוצאים בשאלה (Hillel)**
  - **Role:** Emergency shelters, transitional housing, scholarships, mentoring, and community for young adults leaving the Ultra-Orthodox community.
- **מכון רקמן (Rackman Center — Bar-Ilan University Faculty of Law)**
  - **Role:** Legal aid and representation for women in family court and divorce proceedings, crucial for securing civil Family Court jurisdiction (*תפיסת סמכות*) before Rabbinical Court.
- **נעמת (Na'amat)**
  - **Role:** Legal counseling and representation for women in divorce, custody, and workplace rights.
- **עוגן (Ogen — Social Loan Fund)**
  - **Role:** Low-interest social loans and financial mentoring (useful when needing to fund legal representation in divorce or transition costs).
- **מכינת גבע — גשר בין עולמות (Mechinat Geva)**
  - **Role:** 6-month pre-military academy (*מכינה קדם-צבאית*) bringing together former Haredim and secular Israelis for physical, cultural, and social preparation before IDF service.
