# Sinai Border Crossing & Travel Logistics

Practical, field-tested guidance synthesized from real travelers and verified posts from the public Sinai community group (*"אוהבי סיני - ללא צנזורה"*, Group ID `197506064300055`). Every factual guideline is linked to its source post ID and timestamp.

## 1. Border Crossing Procedures & Fees

### Step-by-Step Border Walkthrough
1. **Israeli Terminal (Taba Crossing / מעבר טאבה)**:
   - **Israeli Exit Fee**: **~115–120 NIS per person** *(Ref: FB Post ID #2036061787111131, 2026-09-23)*. Can be pre-paid online via Milgam (מילגם) to bypass counter queues.
   - Pass Israeli passport control and security inspection.
   - Walk through the duty-free pavilion across the border strip to the Egyptian terminal.
2. **Egyptian Terminal**:
   - Fill out the white arrival entry card (keep a personal pen accessible).
   - **Pay the Egyptian Border Tax (CRITICAL)**: **$120 USD per person**, payable in **cash US Dollars** *(Ref: FB Post ID #2036835987033711, 2026-09-24; #2036061787111131, 2026-09-23)*.
     - **Bill Quality**: Cash must consist of clean, crisp, newer-series US dollar bills without tears, stains, or ink stamps *(Ref: FB Post ID #2036835987033711, 2026-09-24)*.
     - **Exact Change**: Egyptian cashier booths frequently lack or withhold change; bringing exact $120 bills per person prevents costly arguments or delays *(Ref: FB Post ID #2036835987033711, 2026-09-24)*.
     - **Advance Cash Withdrawal**: Travelers must withdraw USD in advance before reaching the terminal (e.g. from USD-dispensing Bank Leumi ATMs in Eilat) as border exchange rates are poor *(Ref: FB Post ID #2037266793657297, 2026-09-24)*.
     - **MANDATORY RECEIPT PRESERVATION**: Keep the Egyptian fee payment receipt inside your passport for the entire duration of your trip. Border control inspects this receipt upon exit; travelers who lost it were forced to repay the full $120 USD *(Ref: FB Post ID #2036835987033711, 2026-09-24)*.
   - Pass Egyptian passport control (stamped with the free 14-day Sinai Resort Permit).
   - Luggage X-ray screening and customs check.
   - Exit through the terminal gates to the taxi ranks.

### Passport & Visa Rules
- **Passport Validity**: Requires a minimum of 3–6 months validity remaining.
- **Sinai Resort Permit (Stamp)**: Free 14-day permit stamped at the border, covering the coastal strip from Taba down through Bir Sweir, Ras Shaitan, Nuweiba, Dahab, Sharm el-Sheikh, and inland to St. Catherine.
- **Full Egyptian Visa**: Required only if traveling west across the Suez Canal (Cairo, Alexandria, Luxor) or staying longer than 14 days. Must be arranged in advance.

## 2. The Car Dilemma: Entering with an Israeli Vehicle vs. Taxis

### Entering with an Israeli Car
- **Pros**: Private AC transport, flexibility to move between multiple destinations (e.g. Dahab + Nuweiba).
- **Cons**:
  - **Border Delays**: Adds 1 to 2.5 hours at the border for vehicle registration, chassis verification, and document processing *(Ref: FB Post ID #2036941127023197, 2026-09-24)*.
  - **Extra Financial Cost**: Costs an extra 80–120 USD (Arabic translation of vehicle registration, temporary Egyptian license plates, mandatory Egyptian liability insurance, road taxes) *(Ref: FB Post ID #2036941127023197, 2026-09-24)*.
  - **Ownership Restriction**: The vehicle must be registered under the driver's name, or accompanied by an official notarized power of attorney in Arabic/English. Leased or company vehicles require explicit written authorization from the leasing firm.
- **CRITICAL Dashcam Prohibition**: Dashboard cameras (מצלמות דרך) are strictly banned and heavily scrutinized by Egyptian military and security personnel. **All dashcams, camera mounts, and recording devices must be removed from the vehicle before arriving at the border** *(Ref: FB Post ID #2036941127023197, 2026-09-24; #2037943980256245, 2026-09-25)*.

### Standard Recommendation for Most Vacationers
Experienced travelers strongly advise **parking in Eilat and using local Sinai taxis**:
- Completely eliminates vehicle bureaucracy and long border checkpoint waits *(Ref: FB Post ID #2036941127023197, 2026-09-24)*.
- **Luggage Porters**: Bedouin porters with large wheelbarrows wait at the Egyptian border exit to transport heavy bags directly to the taxi ranks for a modest tip (~20–50 NIS or 50–100 EGP) *(Ref: FB Post ID #2036941127023197, 2026-09-24)*.

## 3. Eilat Long-Term Parking (חניון לטווח ארוך - Most Common Choice)

Leaving your car in a designated long-term parking lot in Eilat and crossing on foot is the default choice for Israeli visitors.

### Primary Choice: חניון לטווח ארוך מעבר טאבה (חניון מנחם בגין)
- **Location**: Situated directly opposite the entrance to the Israeli Taba terminal.
- **Payment & Pricing**: Regulated municipal parking managed via Pango / Cello apps (~25–30 NIS per calendar day).
- **Capacity & Peak Bottlenecks**:
  - This lot has several hundred spaces but **regularly fills to capacity** during Israeli holidays (Sukkot, Pesach, Rosh Hashana, Shavuot) and peak summer weekends *(Ref: FB Post ID #2037878773596099, 2026-09-25)*.
  - *"מישהו יודע אם יש מקום בחניה לטווח ארוך ?"* is one of the most common live queries in the group *(Ref: FB Post ID #2037878773596099, 2026-09-25)*.
  - When the electronic signs display *"חניון מלא"* (Lot Full), police and security divert incoming traffic back toward Eilat.

### Designated Overflow Lot: חניון שדה התעופה הישן (חניון הטרמינל)
- **Location**: Central Eilat, on the grounds of the decommissioned Eilat airport.
- **Role**: The official municipal long-term overflow lot when the border lot is full.
- **Connecting Transit to Border**:
  - **Egged Bus Line 30**: Direct public bus running from central Eilat / airport directly to the Taba border terminal (~5 NIS).
  - **Eilat City Taxi**: Readily available throughout Eilat (~40–50 NIS to the Taba border terminal).

### Other Options: Southern Beach Lots
- Open gravel parking spaces along Highway 90 near southern beaches (Almog Beach / Coral Beach). Free of charge, but unshaded and unmonitored; ensure you never park on red-and-white curbs or obstruct emergency routes.

## 4. Customs, Luggage & Prohibited Items

- **Alcohol**: Permitted up to **1 liter of spirits or 1 bottle of wine per adult** *(Ref: FB Post ID #2037761370274506, 2026-09-25)*. Bringing your own alcohol from Israel or the border duty-free is standard practice, as local Sinai alcohol is notoriously weak ("כמו מיץ פטל") *(Ref: FB Post ID #2037761370274506, 2026-09-25)*.
- **Laptops & Tablets**: Completely permitted. Standard airport-style X-ray scan; travelers routinely bring laptops for remote work without complications *(Ref: FB Post ID #2037716263612350, 2026-09-25)*.
- **Snorkeling Gear**: Masks, snorkels, and fins pass through customs freely *(Ref: FB Post ID #2037261343657842, 2026-09-24)*.
- **Religious Items**: Tefillin, tallit, and prayer books are permitted and respected; border security may inspect them on the scanner *(Ref: FB Post ID #2037387420311901, 2026-09-24)*.
- **STRICTLY PROHIBITED ITEMS**:
  - **Drones**: Drones of all kinds are strictly illegal in Egypt. Carrying one leads to instant confiscation, interrogation, and potential detention.
  - **Camping Gas Canisters (בלוני גז לגזייה)**: Strictly prohibited at border screening for hazardous materials safety *(Ref: FB Post ID #2036018023782174, 2026-09-23)*.
  - **Weapons & Spent Ammunition**: Thoroughly check all bags and former military backpacks. Even a single spent shell casing (תרמיל) will cause immediate arrest and detention at Egyptian customs.
  - **Narcotics**: Complete zero tolerance under Egyptian law.

## 5. Money, Currency Exchange & Cash Budgeting

- **Cash is Essential**: Camps, beach kiosks, and local taxi drivers operate strictly in cash; working credit card terminals do not exist in remote camps *(Ref: FB Post ID #2037828863601090, 2026-09-25; #2036807723703204, 2026-09-24)*.
- **Required Currencies**:
  - **US Dollars ($120 USD)**: Required in cash for the Egyptian border entry tax *(Ref: FB Post ID #2036835987033711, 2026-09-24)*.
  - **Egyptian Pounds (EGP / לירות מצריות)**: Required for daily spending (food, drinks, tips, camps) *(Ref: FB Post ID #2036807723703204, 2026-09-24)*.
- **Where to Exchange / Withdraw**:
  - Withdraw US Dollars in Eilat before heading to the border (e.g. Bank Leumi ATMs in Eilat offer USD withdrawals) *(Ref: FB Post ID #2037266793657297, 2026-09-24)*.
  - Exchange Shekels/USD into Egyptian Pounds at the official bank branch inside the Egyptian border terminal.
  - Official bank ATMs exist at the Taba border terminal and in Dahab/Nuweiba city centers, but camp ATMs are non-existent.
- **Estimated Cash Needs**:
  - **Daily Meals & Drinks (per couple)**: ~1,500–3,000 EGP per day covers hearty meals (fish, chicken, fresh salads, shakshuka), juices, and Bedouin tea.
  - **Lodging**: Basic hushas cost 70–120 NIS/night; AC beachfront rooms with private baths cost 200–350 NIS/night *(Ref: FB Post ID #2037883970262246, 2026-09-25; #2037176806999629, 2026-09-24)*.

## 6. SIM Cards & Connectivity

- **Local Carriers**:
  - **Vodafone Egypt**: Recommended. Widest, most consistent 4G reception along the coastal highway and in Dahab *(Ref: FB Post ID #2037912336926076, 2026-09-25)*.
  - **Orange Egypt**: Solid alternative with fast speeds in Nuweiba and Dahab.
  - **WE**: Budget state carrier, good in urban pockets.
- **Where to Buy**:
  - Kiosks and vendor stalls right outside the Egyptian border terminal exit *(Ref: FB Post ID #2036796950370948, 2026-09-24)*.
  - Official operator branches in Nuweiba or Dahab *(Ref: FB Post ID #2037912336926076, 2026-09-25)*.
- **Activation Check**: Ask the vendor to register the SIM with your passport, activate the data package, and test browsing on your phone before departing *(Ref: FB Post ID #2036023760448267, 2026-09-23)*.

## 7. Taxis & Transport in Sinai

- **Taxi Availability**: Taxis and minibuses operate 24/7 immediately outside the Egyptian terminal exit.
- **Rule #1: Negotiate and Fix Price Upfront**: Always confirm the total price, destination, and currency before loading bags or boarding.
- **Typical Rates (Subject to seasonal inflation)**:
  - Taba → Bir Sweir: ~400–600 EGP (~35–50 NIS)
  - Taba → Ras Shaitan / Al-Mahash: ~600–900 EGP (~50–75 NIS)
  - Taba → Nuweiba: ~700–1,000 EGP (~60–85 NIS)
  - Taba → Dahab: ~1,500–2,200 EGP (~120–180 NIS)
- **Active Operators**: Services like Abu Sweilim Sinai Taxis (מוניות אבו סוילם) offer coordinated rides and private minivans across Taba, Nuweiba, Dahab, and St. Catherine *(Ref: FB Post ID #2036949690355674, 2026-09-24; #2036367287080581, 2026-09-23)*.
- **Sharing Taxis**: Travelers constantly organize shared taxis between Dahab/Nuweiba and the border to split expenses *(Ref: FB Post ID #2037871093596867, 2026-09-25; #2036103257106984, 2026-09-23)*.
