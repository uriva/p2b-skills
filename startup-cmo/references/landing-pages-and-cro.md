# Landing Pages, Zero-Auth Demos & CRO

## Eliminate Auth Walls Before Value

The single highest-leverage conversion win on most software/AI landing pages is letting visitors experience the core product **before** creating an account.

- Replace `"Sign up to try"` with a 1-click launch into a pre-filled live chat, interactive sandbox, or instant tool runner.
- If you have a directory/launcher page (e.g. launching a template, skill, or workflow), wire it straight to the live product with pre-filled context instead of gating it behind OAuth/magic-link login. Measure both CTA click rate and `Signin Error` drop to confirm the lift.
- **Replace dead-end empty-account states with a 30-second 1-click Quickstart:** When signups and API-key creations are healthy yet external production usage sits near 0%, inspect the post-login empty screen. Replace text instructions (`"Go to Settings to create a key"`) with 1-click workspace + key creation, a 1-click `"Send sample event"` button that demonstrates the flagship differentiator live (e.g. live streaming + automatic secret redaction), and pre-filled multi-language copy-paste snippets.

## Visual & Copy Discipline

- **Lead with the concrete differentiator:** Spell out what your product actually does that a generic browser chatbot or DIY script cannot (for example: its own computer/VM, real phone line, group chat presence, proactive outreach, zero self-hosting).
- **One cohesive visual palette:** Keep primary and secondary CTAs inside one unified brand color system — avoid jarring multi-color clashes (like random neon green buttons beside blue brand accents).
- **Equal visual weight for primary/secondary pairs:** When showing two sibling actions (e.g. "Try Now" + "See Demo"), keep button sizing, padding, and hierarchy balanced.
- **Cut clutter ruthlessly:** Strip generic SaaS comparison tables, repetitive fine-print disclaimers under buttons, vague buzzwords, and decorative fluff. Keep hero CTA bars tight (`<= 3` actions: 1 primary trial/try CTA + at most 2 secondary reference links) so visitors never hit choice paralysis. Show real product artifacts (live interactive previews, concrete prompt chips, crisp editorial illustrations).
- **Optimize mobile first and inspect real screenshots:** Always render and inspect real mobile (`375x812`) and desktop (`1280x900`) screenshots of your landing page, sign-in wall, and `/pricing`. Verify (1) the primary CTA sits **above the mobile fold**, (2) primary OAuth buttons (like Google with its 4-color "G" mark) have unmistakable contrast vs secondary email forms, and (3) zero horizontal overflow occurs. On channels where users arrive on mobile (WhatsApp, X/Twitter, Reddit mobile), a clean header or sticky mobile CTA pill with a value-driven label (e.g. `"No Watermark"` instead of vague `"Pro"`) drives a major share of pricing visits.
- **Preserve user intent across auth & channel fallbacks:** Store pending queries (`?say=`) and checkout intents (`?subscribe=`) in `localStorage` with a short TTL (not just `sessionStorage`, which drops across mobile OAuth redirects and email magic-link tabs). When offering a no-sign-in fallback channel (like WhatsApp), pre-fill the user's pending query right into the link (`?text=<encoded query>`) so they never retype — and when expanding to new international cities where local phone numbers (`+49`, etc.) require slow regulatory provisioning, route international/English traffic straight to an active verified `+1` line with a pre-filled city prompt rather than stalling market validation.
- **Turn delivered outputs into a viral + monetized share loop:** On media/artifact products, include 1-tap `Download` + branded `Share` links right next to every delivered output (fixing missing mobile `<video>` download buttons while routing shared links to a clean player page with a prominent upgrade CTA and an interactive "Try it yourself" search bar).
- **Render invite auth on the canonical URL and keep CTAs above the mobile fold:** Redirecting `/item/:id` -> `/login?item=:id` adds a spinner flash and causes visitors who copy the browser bar to share URLs lacking dynamic OG previews. Render auth directly on the canonical invite URL (and also serve OG meta for `/login?item=...`), clamp long user-generated descriptions behind `"Read more"` so primary login buttons sit above the mobile fold (`390x844`), and isolate numeric/English social-proof badges with `dir="ltr"` inside `dir="auto"` RTL content.
- **Audit empty states on creator & venue artifacts:** Every printable QR poster, public gallery, or shared item page linked from onboarding emails must work cleanly with 0 items (include a white-framed scannable QR code, ink-friendly `@media print` styling, and a 1-tap "Join / Create first item" CTA — never a dead `"No items yet"` screen).

## Mobile WebViews & Onboarding Form Killers

- **Social in-app WebViews break Google OAuth (`403 disallowed_useragent`):** Detect `FBAN|FBAV|Instagram|TikTok` user agents and lead with email 6-digit OTP instead of Google OAuth. Always use `type="text" inputMode="numeric" autoComplete="one-time-code"` (never `type="number"`, which strips leading zeros), wrap in `<form onSubmit>`, and provide Resend + Change email controls.
- **Survive mobile photo-picker tab reloads and flaky uploads:** Returning from an Android/iOS camera or gallery picker frequently reloads the browser tab. Persist in-progress onboarding forms in `localStorage` with a 24h TTL (and never put async query fields in a form-reset dependency array!), use standard `accept="image/*"` (never invalid `image/*;capture=camera`), retry image compression without WebWorkers on WebViews, and keep non-essential cover images optional on first creator setup.
- **Eliminate post-login dead ends on every CTA:** Test signing in from every landing/use-case CTA (`?next=...` validated against open redirects) and direct `/login` visits so newly signed-in users never remain stranded on `/login`.

## Turning Blog Readers Into Product Users

Technical blog posts often get strong search and community traffic while converting at `<2%` because they end with a cold generic `/login` button.

- Add a **lightweight top strip** linking directly to the flagship try-now experience (`location="top_strip"`).
- Replace generic bottom signup boxes with a **product-first bottom CTA** featuring equal-sized Try Now + Live Demo buttons (`location="blog_post_cta"`, `location="bottom_cta"`).
- Keep the tone editorial and helpful — never pop up aggressive modals that alienate developer readers.

## Web Engineering Gotchas That Silently Break CRO

- **Prerender / SSR hydration with query params:** On statically prerendered pages (`?ref=...`, `?utm_source=...`, `?token=...`), never read `location.search` during the initial render pass — runtime DOM mismatch corrupts hydration and breaks click handlers. Read query params after mount and preserve SSR-safe fallback links.
- **Audit built HTML for duplicate `<link rel="canonical">` and `<meta name="description">` tags:** Prerender plugins often append per-route head elements instead of replacing hardcoded homepage tags in `index.html`, causing every subpage (`/compare`, `/pricing`, `/guides/*`, `/blog/*`) to tell Google its canonical URL is `/`. Move route-specific `<title>`, `<meta name="description">`, `og:*`, and `<link rel="canonical">` into one per-route registry and verify exact single tags in the built HTML.
- **Check trailing-slash prerender routing and static-file collisions on static hosts:** On static hosts with an SPA wildcard rewrite (`/* /index.html 200`), `/pricing` often falls through to homepage HTML while only `/pricing/` serves `pricing/index.html`. Always curl both `/route` and `/route/` live, align `sitemap.xml` and canonicals to the URL form returning prerendered HTML, and add explicit route rewrites ahead of the wildcard rule. Mark links to static non-HTML root files (like `/llms.txt`) `target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"` so prerender link crawlers do not collide (`EEXIST`) trying to emit a directory for them.
- **Visually inspect generated OG social cards (`1200x630`):** When generating social preview PNGs programmatically, always open and visually inspect the rendered image for text clipping, badge overlap, and contrast before shipping.
- **Explain identity gates in terms of user value + privacy:** Generic `"Required to secure your account"` SMS/phone walls routinely cause 50%+ signup drop-off. State plainly why the check exists (keeping shared domain/service deliverability spam-free), guarantee the number is never used for marketing or shared, and label the button with the reward on the other side (e.g. `"Unlock Free 30-Day Inbox"`).
- **Trim auth inputs & audit overly strict domain allowlists:** Always `.trim()` email and verification-code inputs before validation so trailing spaces never reject valid signups. Audit whether personal-email-only allowlists are silently bouncing B2B/startup/HN developers typing work or custom domains when phone verification already blocks abuse farms.
- **Progressive-enhancement hero forms:** Add native `action="/app" method="get"` and `name="..."` attributes on hero search/prompt forms so fast submissions before JS hydration on slow mobile networks still navigate cleanly instead of reloading `/?`.
- **Never prerender a blank loading spinner on `/docs` or SEO pages:** Scope client-side loading spinners strictly to dynamic sub-sections (like OpenAPI endpoint lists) so static HTML immediately exposes the heading, quickstart, and pricing/token CTA to search crawlers and users.
- **Verify past CDN caches and silent build failures:** Static hosts often keep serving the previous build if a deploy fails and cache HTML for several minutes (`s-maxage=300`). Always check live deploy status and bundle hashes (`?v=<timestamp>`) before announcing a launch or evaluating metrics.
- **Respect experiment bake windows:** Do not redesign or churn a landing page or CTA that changed `<24h` ago unless telemetry shows a broken click path, hydration bug, or JS exception. Small samples (`<30-50` visits) fluctuate wildly with traffic source mix.
